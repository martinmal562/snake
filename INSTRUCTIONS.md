🐍 SNAKE
Objective:

Eat as much food as possible to get the highest score without crashing into the walls or your own tail.

Controls
Keyboard: W, A, S, D to change the direction of the snake.

On-Screen: Click the arrow buttons displayed below the game screen.

Rules
The game starts when you click the start button.

The snake moves forward continuously.

Each piece of food eaten increases the snake's length and adds points to your Score.

Game Over: The game ends immediately if the snake's head hits the edge of the screen or any part of its own body.

🪓 HANGMAN
Objective:

Guess the hidden word before you run out of attempts and the gallows are fully built.

Controls
Keyboard: Type letters directly on your physical keyboard.

On-Screen: Click the keys on the virtual QWERTY keyboard layout provided below the game area.

Rules
Click start to generate a random secret word. Blank lines will show you how many letters the word has.

Guess a letter you think is in the word:

Correct Guess: The letter appears in its correct position(s) within the word.

Incorrect Guess: The letter is disabled on the keyboard, and a new part of the hangman drawing appears.

Winning: Successfully reveal all the letters in the word before the drawing is finished.

Losing: The hangman is fully drawn, and the correct word is revealed.**
